# A Minor Guitar Course

15 JSON exercises for a single-note guitar practice app.

- Tuning: standard E-A-D-G-B-E
- String numbering: 6 = low E, 1 = high E
- Beats may be integers, eighth-note halves, or triplet decimals
- Chords are encoded as arpeggios; there are no simultaneous notes

## Exercises

1. **A Minor 01 — Find the A Notes** — 55 BPM, 7 notes (`01_find_the_a_notes.json`)
2. **A Minor 02 — Open-Position Scale** — 60 BPM, 15 notes (`02_open_position_scale.json`)
3. **A Minor 03 — Fifth-Position Scale** — 60 BPM, 33 notes (`03_fifth_position_scale.json`)
4. **A Minor 04 — Groups of Three** — 65 BPM, 45 notes (`04_groups_of_three.json`)
5. **A Minor 05 — Groups of Four** — 60 BPM, 56 notes (`05_groups_of_four.json`)
6. **A Minor 06 — Diatonic Thirds** — 60 BPM, 30 notes (`06_scale_in_thirds.json`)
7. **A Minor 07 — String Crossing** — 65 BPM, 30 notes (`07_string_crossing.json`)
8. **A Minor 08 — Alternate-Picking Eighths** — 75 BPM, 66 notes (`08_alternate_picking_eighths.json`)
9. **A Minor 09 — Alternate-Picking Triplets** — 60 BPM, 66 notes (`09_alternate_picking_triplets.json`)
10. **A Minor 10 — Am Chord Tones** — 60 BPM, 17 notes (`10_am_chord_tones.json`)
11. **A Minor 11 — All Diatonic Triads** — 58 BPM, 35 notes (`11_diatonic_triads.json`)
12. **A Minor 12 — All Diatonic Seventh Chords** — 55 BPM, 49 notes (`12_diatonic_seventh_chords.json`)
13. **A Minor 13 — Short Melody** — 68 BPM, 16 notes (`13_short_melody.json`)
14. **A Minor 14 — A Harmonic Minor** — 55 BPM, 15 notes (`14_harmonic_minor.json`)
15. **A Minor 15 — E7 Resolves to Am** — 55 BPM, 28 notes (`15_e7_to_am_resolution.json`)

## Suggested use

Start with exercises 1–3. Add one sequencing exercise at a time, then study the chord arpeggios. Increase tempo only after playing cleanly and evenly.
